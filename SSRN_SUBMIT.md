# SSRN submission checklist (the author does this; about twenty minutes)

Target: SSRN **Computer Science Research Network** (Elsevier). Papers are posted as PDFs, get a Crossref DOI, and are indexed by Google Scholar. Classification by the SSRN team normally takes one to three business days. Posting on SSRN does not prevent a later arXiv posting or the ARR submission (ACL has no anonymity period; keep the review version anonymous and tick "preprint available" on the ARR form).

1. **Account.** Sign in at ssrn.com with a complete author profile; add co-authors by email so the paper appears on their profiles (Wang, Peng, Ananiadou, Tsujii).
2. **File.** Upload `release/from_memory_to_belief_preprint_v5.pdf`. It already shows the title, all five authors with affiliations, the abstract, and the required **AI disclosure** paragraph under the abstract. Do not upload the anonymous review version.
3. **Metadata.**
   - Title: *From Memory to Belief: A Survey of State Maintenance and Belief Revision in LLM Decision Agents*
   - Date written: the date you submit.
   - Abstract: paste the abstract from the PDF, then append the AI disclosure paragraph (SSRN requires the disclosure to appear with the abstract as well as in the PDF).
   - Keywords, one at a time: large language models; LLM agents; belief state; belief revision; memory; reinforcement learning; credit assignment; uncertainty; evaluation; survey.
   - Network: Computer Science Research Network; suggest the Artificial Intelligence eJournal. Classifications are set by SSRN and cannot be changed later by the author.
   - Related material: link `https://github.com/jiminHuang/belief-state-survey` in the abstract's last line or the comments field.
4. **Declaration of interests (paste into the SSRN form; it is also printed in the PDF).**

   > Jimin Huang and Xueqing Peng are affiliated with The Fin AI, which produced several of the finance benchmarks discussed in this survey, among them FinBen and InvestorBench; these were screened, included, and described under the same protocol as every other paper. The authors declare no other financial or personal relationships with people or organisations that could be perceived to have influenced the work reported in this paper. This research was supported by the NVIDIA Academic Grant Program (32K A100 GPU-hours on Brev); the funder had no role in the design of the review, the screening protocol, the analysis, or the decision to publish.

5. **Copyright.** You keep copyright; SSRN gets a non-exclusive right to distribute. Nothing here blocks ACL's later licence.
6. **After posting.** Send the SSRN DOI (and the abstract-page URL) to the agent, or edit by hand: it goes into the citation block generated by `lit_search/make_readme.py` and into the footnote in `survey_tex/main_preprint.tex` (`\artifacturl`). Rebuild the preprint PDF, then upload the revised PDF to SSRN as a new revision and push the repository.
7. **Artifacts.** SSRN hosts the paper only. Deposit `release/from_memory_to_belief_artifacts_v5.zip` on Zenodo as a Dataset/Software record (metadata in `release/zenodo_metadata.json`) and cite the SSRN DOI there as the related publication.
