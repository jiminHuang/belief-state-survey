\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This survey took the belief state apart into its state, transition, and likelihood terms and asked of every included system which terms it has and where each comes from. The field has given the agent a state, from the trace to stores, filters, and world models, and a transition, with credit made denser at every level below the state; it has built more than twenty ways to measure belief without a shared one, and the likelihood is still supplied from outside. Three findings carry across the axes. The representations that can be falsified are not the ones the agent can reason about; the mechanisms that revise beliefs do not route the error to the claim that produced it; and the two methods that credit the belief from the environment's own returns score a binary belief and stand alone. The next step is precise: an agent whose calibrated written belief is scored by its own matured predictions, evaluated where the latent state is known.
