\ifnature That something is a testable belief, and this is the shift the survey argues for. A memory records what has happened, whereas a belief is a hypothesis about what is true of the world, which a prediction exposes to evidence, a test supports or falsifies, and a revision updates (Fig.~\ref{fig:loop}). The test can take many forms (Section~\ref{sec:formulation}). This review follows three parts, a \emph{belief state} that says what is believed and how firmly, a \emph{revision} that carries it across an action, and a \emph{test} that scores it against the next observation, with the Bayes filter as their principled formalisation \citep{astrom1965optimal,kaelbling1998planning,thrun2005probabilistic,friston2010free}. The line runs through memory research, not around it. A store that attaches a confidence and an update rule to its claims already has the first two parts \citep{zhang2024memory,luo2026storage}, and what makes it a belief is the third, a test that connects the hypothesis to evidence that can support or falsify it. Memory and uncertainty research gave the agent a state with a confidence \citep{park2023generative,shinn2023reflexion,lin2022teaching,kadavath2022language}, and post-training taught it to revise, rewarding the response, the step, or the action \citep{ouyang2022training,lightman2023lets,zhou2024archer,zhang2026reasoning}.
