Until 2026 every such method took its test from outside the environment. CBM trains with belief-state rewards from symbolic verification and cuts belief-management failures by about 71\% \citep{xu2026should}, Agent-BRACE trains belief model and policy jointly with verbalised certainty as the belief's output \citep{singh2026agent}, and BOND distils a Bayesian teacher's posterior into an 8B student \citep{cui2026distilling}. VAGEN rewards a written state prediction against the simulator's state \citep{wang2025vagen}, T3 truncates rollouts by a belief deviation anchored to the true hypothesis \citep{zou2025t3}, IGPO and InfoPO reward a turn by the belief update it causes toward the known answer \citep{wang2025information,kong2026infopo}, Dyna-Think, SPA, and RLVR-World train state prediction as a loss or verifiable reward \citep{yu2025dynathink,chen2025why,wu2025rlvrworld}, DEL-ToM scores each belief-update step with a process belief model \citep{wu2025deltom}, and CORE and GAVEL train a probabilistic belief against gold slots or held-out placements \citep{zhang2026toward,wang2026gavel}. Each needs the answer or the latent state at training time.
