\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Table~\ref{tab:surveys} places this survey among sixteen neighbouring ones by which term of the belief each supplies. Memory surveys \citep{zhang2024memory,hu2025memory,luo2026storage} supply the state term and its persistence, the newest with a probability or revision operator on each item; what they do not ask is what evidence would retire an item, which is the test. Uncertainty surveys \citep{xia2025uncertainty,lin2026uncertainty} supply a confidence on the state, but attached to an output and neither carried forward nor revised. Reward and credit surveys \citep{wu2025rewards,zheng2025prm,zhang2025landscape,zhang2026reasoning} supply the transition and the learning signal, following credit down to tokens, steps, and memory operations, and stop at the action. World-model and long-horizon surveys \citep{li2026bridging,chen2026horizon} supply a learned transition and likelihood whose state the agent cannot read. Evaluation, self-evolution, forecasting, and finance surveys \citep{yehudai2025survey,gao2025survey,xu2026llm,dong2025finance} score the outcome and treat the belief as one component among many. Two recent surveys formalise the belief itself as a Bayes filter, for complex problem solving \citep{cai2026complex} and for proactive service agents \citep{tang2026proactive}, but update it at inference time in one setting, so the test never reaches it as a learning signal. Each strand holds one term or one setting; none follows the three terms across agents into learning and evaluation, which is the question Section~\ref{sec:formulation} turns into a definition.
