Table~\ref{tab:surveys} places this survey among sixteen neighbouring ones by which part of the belief each supplies. Memory surveys supply the belief state \citep{zhang2024memory,hu2025memory,luo2026storage}, uncertainty surveys a confidence on it \citep{xia2025uncertainty,lin2026uncertainty}, and reward and credit surveys the revision and learning signal \citep{wu2025rewards,zheng2025prm,zhang2025landscape,zhang2026reasoning}, while world-model, evaluation, and domain surveys treat the belief as one component among many \citep{li2026bridging,chen2026horizon,yehudai2025survey,gao2025survey,xu2026llm,dong2025finance}. Two recent surveys formalise the belief as a Bayes filter, each for one setting at inference time \citep{cai2026complex,tang2026proactive}. None follows the three parts across agents into learning and evaluation (Appendix~\ref{app:full}).
