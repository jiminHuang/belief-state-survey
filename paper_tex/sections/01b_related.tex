\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Table~\ref{tab:surveys} places this survey among fourteen neighbouring ones by which term of the belief each supplies. Memory surveys \citep{zhang2024memory,hu2025memory,luo2026storage} supply the state term and its persistence, and the newest already cover stores that attach a probability or a revision operator to each item, so the line between memory and belief runs inside memory research rather than around it; what they do not ask is what evidence would retire an item, which is the likelihood. Uncertainty surveys \citep{xia2025uncertainty,lin2026uncertainty} supply the confidence on the state: a verbalised probability is a degree of belief and calibration is the test the state term admits, but the probability is attached to an output and neither carried forward nor revised, so they cover the state without the transition. Reward and credit surveys \citep{wu2025rewards,zheng2025prm,zhang2025landscape,zhang2026reasoning} supply the transition and the learning signal, following credit down to tokens, steps, and memory operations, and stop at the action. World-model and long-horizon surveys \citep{li2026bridging,chen2026horizon} supply a learned transition and likelihood whose state the agent cannot read. Evaluation, self-evolution, forecasting, and finance surveys \citep{yehudai2025survey,gao2025survey,xu2026llm,dong2025finance} score the outcome and treat the belief as one component among many. Each strand holds one term; none asks how the three come together in one object, which is the question Section~\ref{sec:formulation} turns into a definition.
