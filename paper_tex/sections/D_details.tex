\section{Further Details}
\label{app:details}

\paragraph{State-level learning signals (Section~\ref{sec:learn:state}).}
Among language-model agents, until 2026 every such method took its test from outside the environment. CBM trains with belief-state rewards from symbolic verification and cuts belief-management failures by about 71\% \citep{xu2026should}, Agent-BRACE trains belief model and policy jointly with verbalised certainty as the belief's output \citep{singh2026agent}, and BOND distils a Bayesian teacher's posterior into an 8B student \citep{cui2026distilling}. VAGEN rewards a written state prediction against the simulator's state \citep{wang2025vagen}, T3 truncates rollouts by a belief deviation anchored to the true hypothesis \citep{zou2025t3}, IGPO and InfoPO reward a turn by the belief update it causes toward the known answer \citep{wang2025information,kong2026infopo}, Dyna-Think, SPA, and RLVR-World train state prediction as a loss or verifiable reward \citep{yu2025dynathink,chen2025why,wu2025rlvrworld}, DEL-ToM scores each belief-update step with a process belief model \citep{wu2025deltom}, and CORE and GAVEL train a probabilistic belief against gold slots or held-out placements \citep{zhang2026toward,wang2026gavel}. Each needs the answer or the latent state at training time. Three systems now take it from the agent's own observations: ReBel rewards each predicate of a written belief that the next observation supports, with the gradient reaching the belief \citep{tang2026rewarding}; ABBEL grades a natural-language belief by how well the prior observation can be reconstructed from it \citep{lidayan2025abbel}; PaW adds a next-observation loss on the policy \citep{lu2026policy}. None carries uncertainty, and the dense form is fragile: the same reward through group-normalised advantages collapses every run, and as an auxiliary loss it is matched by shuffled targets \citep{wang2026dark}.

\paragraph{Belief-level constructs (Section~\ref{sec:eval:constructs}).}
\emph{Calibration over a trajectory} has the most statistics and the least comparability: checkpoint and episode calibration, posterior entropy, and proper scoring rules on time-gated forecasts express one construct four ways, none computed on another paper's system \citep{lin2026uncertainty,singh2026agent,chattopadhayay2026belief,dixon2026belief,ghosh2026finbench,ye2026forecast}; one replicated deficit flipped sign across model versions \citep{joshi2026replication}. \emph{Belief accuracy and revision} are measured against a known answer in a closed world, after a minimal premise edit, and across recall, inference, and change detection \citep{xu2026should,dhanda2026deltalogic,nguyen2026dynamic}, against human annotation or a hidden ledger \citep{myakala2026beliefshift,chao2026stale,hao2026memops,yang2026groupmembench,srivastava2026constructing}, a probed spatial map or a screenplay's state \citep{zhang2026theory,tian2026stage}, and theory-of-mind benchmarks \citep{kim2023fantom,bawatneh2026omnitom,wang2026mindgames}. \emph{Memory validity} is scored per operation or by penalising invalidated memories \citep{chen2025halumem,wu2024longmemeval,liu2026worldmemarena,uddin2026recall}. \emph{Bayesian coherence} compares a belief trajectory with an ideal updater: total-variation distance to the exact posterior \citep{samanta2026bayesbench}, a martingale test on successive beliefs \citep{he2025martingale}, and the correlation between the model's update and the log-odds of the evidence \citep{imran2025are,yuan2025do}. The \emph{belief--action gap} is the youngest construct: STOCKTAKE scores the agent against an exact Bayes filter on the same observations \citep{deb2026stocktake}, the Werewolf audit reports action--belief consistency \citep{gao2026auditing}, and SimpleToM separates inferring a belief from applying it \citep{gu2024simpletom}.

\paragraph{A further open problem: belief under governance (Section~\ref{sec:challenges}).}
Regulated domains add auditability, which only fixed-space filters provide, and LLM-proposed spaces are the candidate bridge \citep{six2026learning,soru2026semantic,chen2026beliefcalibrated,dixon2026model}.
