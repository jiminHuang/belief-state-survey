\section{The Test as a Metric: Evaluating Belief Apart from Outcome}
\label{sec:evaluation}

\ifnature\input{fig_metrics}\fi

A correct answer can follow from a correct belief, a wrong belief that did not matter, or a belief revised too late, and outcome metrics cannot separate the three, which is why stronger backbones do not yield stronger trading agents \citep{xie2025finben,li2024investorbench}. Earlier agent benchmarks score task success or recall \citep{shridhar2020alfworld,liu2023agentbench,yao2024taubench}. Recent work added belief-level metrics, each defining its own and each measuring one part from outside (calibration the belief state, accuracy the revision, Bayesian coherence the test, the belief--action gap whether the policy uses any). Fig.~\ref{fig:metrics}\appx{} aligns them by construct, and Table~\ref{tab:benchmarks} in the appendix catalogues the benchmarks.

\subsection{Constructs}
\label{sec:eval:constructs}

\ifnature\input{eval_constructs}\else
Appendix~\ref{app:constructs} discusses each construct in full. \emph{Calibration over a trajectory} has the most statistics and the least comparability, and its one replicated deficit flipped sign across model versions \citep{joshi2026replication}. \emph{Belief accuracy and revision} are scored against a known answer, a human annotation, or a hidden ledger \citep{xu2026should,myakala2026beliefshift,chao2026stale}, and \emph{memory validity} per operation \citep{chen2025halumem}. \emph{Bayesian coherence} compares a belief trajectory with an ideal updater \citep{samanta2026bayesbench,he2025martingale}. The \emph{belief--action gap} is the youngest construct, and only STOCKTAKE scores the agent against an exact Bayes filter on the same observations \citep{deb2026stocktake}.
\fi

Every construct beyond calibration needs a latent state to compare against. Controlled environments provide it \citep{deb2026stocktake,xu2026should,alaswad2026why,wang2026convfinre}, long-horizon simulators know it but report belief through proxies \citep{he2026ycbench,zhang2026retailbenchx,han2026can}, and historical replay provides neither \citep{herculean2026}. 

\insight{5}{\textbf{Belief cannot be scored by outcome.} Seven constructs, more than twenty metrics, almost no cross-paper comparison, and only the fair oracle scores belief and action on the same episodes. Progress on belief will be measurable only where the latent state is known, so controlled environments, not replay, are where the next results should come from.}
