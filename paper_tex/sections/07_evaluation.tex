\label{sec:evaluation}
\ifnature\else\input{tab_levels}\fi

\input{fig_metrics}

A correct answer can follow from a correct belief, a wrong belief that did not matter, or a belief revised too late, and outcome metrics such as task success \citep{shridhar2020alfworld,liu2023agentbench,yao2024taubench} cannot separate the three. Recent work added belief-level metrics, each defining its own and each measuring one part from outside (calibration the belief state, accuracy the revision, Bayesian coherence the test, the belief--action gap whether the policy uses any). Fig.~\ref{fig:metrics} aligns them by construct, and Table~\ref{tab:benchmarks} in the appendix catalogues the benchmarks.

\subsection{Constructs}
\label{sec:eval:constructs}

\ifnature\input{eval_constructs}\else
Appendix~\ref{app:constructs} discusses each construct in full. \emph{Calibration over a trajectory} has the most statistics and the least comparability. Checkpoint and episode calibration, posterior entropy, and proper scoring rules express one construct four ways, none computed on another paper's system \citep{lin2026uncertainty,singh2026agent,dixon2026belief,ye2026forecast}, and its one replicated deficit flipped sign across model versions \citep{joshi2026replication}. \emph{Belief accuracy and revision} are scored against a known answer, a human annotation, or a hidden ledger \citep{xu2026should,myakala2026beliefshift,chao2026stale}, after a minimal premise edit \citep{dhanda2026deltalogic,nguyen2026dynamic}, against a probed spatial map \citep{zhang2026theory}, and in theory-of-mind benchmarks \citep{kim2023fantom}. \emph{Memory validity} is scored per operation or by penalising invalidated memories \citep{chen2025halumem,uddin2026recall}. \emph{Bayesian coherence} compares a belief trajectory with an ideal updater, by distance to the exact posterior or a martingale test on successive beliefs \citep{samanta2026bayesbench,he2025martingale}. The \emph{belief--action gap} is the youngest construct, and only STOCKTAKE scores the agent against an exact Bayes filter on the same observations \citep{deb2026stocktake}, the Werewolf audit reports action--belief consistency \citep{gao2026auditing}, and SimpleToM separates inferring a belief from applying it \citep{gu2024simpletom}.
\fi

Every construct beyond calibration needs a latent state to compare against. Controlled environments provide it \citep{deb2026stocktake,xu2026should,alaswad2026why,wang2026convfinre}, long-horizon simulators know it but report belief through proxies \citep{he2026ycbench,zhang2026retailbenchx,han2026can}, and historical replay provides neither \citep{herculean2026}. 

\insight{5}{\textbf{Belief cannot be scored by outcome.} More than twenty metrics share almost no system, and only a known latent state can score belief, so controlled environments, not replay, will carry progress.}
