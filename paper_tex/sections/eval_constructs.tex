\emph{Calibration over a trajectory} has the most statistics and the least comparability. Checkpoint and episode calibration, posterior entropy, and proper scoring rules on time-gated forecasts express one construct four ways, none computed on another paper's system \citep{lin2026uncertainty,singh2026agent,chattopadhayay2026belief,dixon2026belief,ghosh2026finbench,ye2026forecast}, and the one replicated deficit flipped sign across model versions \citep{joshi2026replication}. \emph{Belief accuracy and revision} are measured against a known answer in a closed world, after a minimal premise edit, and across recall, inference, and change detection \citep{xu2026should,dhanda2026deltalogic,nguyen2026dynamic}, against human annotation or a hidden ledger \citep{myakala2026beliefshift,chao2026stale,hao2026memops,yang2026groupmembench,srivastava2026constructing}, a probed spatial map or a screenplay's state \citep{zhang2026theory,tian2026stage}, and theory-of-mind benchmarks \citep{kim2023fantom,bawatneh2026omnitom,wang2026mindgames}. \emph{Memory validity} is scored per operation or by penalising invalidated memories \citep{chen2025halumem,wu2024longmemeval,liu2026worldmemarena,uddin2026recall}. \emph{Bayesian coherence} compares a belief trajectory with an ideal updater, through total-variation distance to the exact posterior \citep{samanta2026bayesbench}, a martingale test on successive beliefs \citep{he2025martingale}, and the correlation between the model's update and the log-odds of the evidence \citep{imran2025are,yuan2025do}. The \emph{belief--action gap} is the youngest construct. STOCKTAKE scores the agent against an exact Bayes filter on the same observations \citep{deb2026stocktake}, the Werewolf audit reports action--belief consistency \citep{gao2026auditing}, and SimpleToM separates inferring a belief from applying it \citep{gu2024simpletom}.
