A learning signal is a test that has become a gradient. Fewer than three in five included papers give the belief any such signal, and for the rest it is built and revised at inference time. Where existing surveys organise post-training by reward source and density \citep{wu2025rewards,zhang2026reasoning,zheng2025prm}, we separate two axes that are usually run together (Fig.~\ref{fig:credit}, Table~\ref{tab:learningmain}), the \emph{credited object}, the quantity that receives the gradient, and the \emph{source of the test}, whatever signs it (humans, a verifier, the task reward, a teacher, a simulator, or the agent's own later observation). A method is at the \emph{state level} if the credited object is a claim about the environment, whatever the source, and the source then decides whether the sign exists outside a simulator.

\subsection{Output and Trajectory}
\label{sec:learn:traj}

Supervised fine-tuning and preference optimisation credit the completed response \citep{ouyang2022training,wei2022finetuned,rafailov2023direct,wang2022self,huang2022large}. Trajectory-level credit began by imitating and searching over agent trajectories \citep{chen2023fireact,zhou2023language,putta2024agent,fu2025agentrefine}, then credited sampled steps through PPO, GRPO, and process reward models \citep{schulman2017proximal,shao2024deepseekmath,lightman2023lets,zheng2025prm}.

\subsection{Interaction}
\label{sec:learn:inter}

Agentic reinforcement learning credits actions over several turns \citep{zhou2024archer,wang2025ragen,zhou2025sweetrl,feng2025group,xiong2025flagtrader}, and the open question is how to make that credit denser without making it wrong, by hierarchical advantages, counterfactual estimators, potential-based shaping, or verifier-structured credit \citep{peng2026hiper,wang2026capo,diao2026hipif,wang2026groupgraph,ren2026iapo,liao2026tcpo,liu2026pica,li2026vict}. The nearest this level comes to the state is a group that credits the write to a memory, from a rewritten case bank to an RL-trained overwrite of a fixed written state \citep{zhou2025memento,zhou2025mem1,yu2025memagent,yan2025memory,sun2026verifiable,yan2026memoryr2}. These credit the \emph{operation}, so a well-executed update to a wrong claim is still rewarded.

\subsection{State}
\label{sec:learn:state}

\ifnature\input{learn_state_oracle}\else
Until 2026 every such method took its test from outside the environment, from symbolic verification \citep{xu2026should}, a calibration target \citep{singh2026agent}, a Bayesian teacher or process belief model \citep{cui2026distilling,wu2025deltom}, the simulator's state \citep{wang2025vagen,yu2025dynathink,chen2025why,wu2025rlvrworld}, or the known answer \citep{zou2025t3,wang2025information,kong2026infopo,zhang2026toward,wang2026gavel}. Each needs the answer or the latent state at training time (Appendix~\ref{app:statecredit}).
\fi Three systems now take it from the agent's own observations. ReBel rewards each predicate of a written belief that the next observation supports, with the gradient reaching the belief \citep{tang2026rewarding}, ABBEL grades a natural-language belief by how well the prior observation can be reconstructed from it \citep{lidayan2025abbel}, and PaW adds a next-observation loss on the policy \citep{lu2026policy}. None carries uncertainty, and the dense form is fragile. The same reward through group-normalised advantages collapses every run, and as an auxiliary loss it is matched by shuffled targets \citep{wang2026dark}.
