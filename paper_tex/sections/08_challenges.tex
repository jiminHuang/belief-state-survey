\section{Open Problems}
\label{sec:challenges}

\paragraph{A calibrated belief with an environment-signed test.}
ReBel signs a written belief with the next observation, but a binary one, and Agent-BRACE carries certainty, but signed by a calibration target. Joining the two without the collapse dense prediction rewards show under group normalisation is the most direct open problem.

\paragraph{Latent beliefs without ground truth.}
Historical data has no oracle, closed world, or annotation. Filter-recovered latents and representation drift are candidate substitutes, unvalidated against each other \citep{dixon2026belief,xue2026representation}, and a benchmark pairing a controlled environment with a replay of the same instrument would settle whether belief-level gains transfer.

\paragraph{Belief under governance.}
Regulated domains add auditability, which only fixed-space filters provide, and LLM-proposed spaces are the candidate bridge \citep{six2026learning,soru2026semantic,chen2026beliefcalibrated,dixon2026model}.
