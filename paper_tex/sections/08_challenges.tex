\section{Open Problems}
\label{sec:challenges}

\paragraph{A calibrated belief with an environment-signed test.}
ReBel signs a written belief with the next observation, but a binary one, and Agent-BRACE carries certainty, but signed by a calibration target. Joining the two without the collapse dense prediction rewards show under group normalisation is the most direct open problem. ReBel's predicates already expose the unit a proper scoring rule needs, so the step is to attach a probability to each and score it against the observation that returns.

\paragraph{Latent beliefs without ground truth.}
Historical data has no oracle or annotation, and its candidate substitutes, filter-recovered latents and representation drift, are unvalidated \citep{dixon2026belief,xue2026representation}; a controlled environment paired with a replay of the same instrument would test whether belief-level gains transfer.

\paragraph{Belief under governance.}
Regulated domains add auditability, which only fixed-space filters provide, and LLM-proposed spaces are the candidate bridge \citep{six2026learning,soru2026semantic,chen2026beliefcalibrated,dixon2026model}.
