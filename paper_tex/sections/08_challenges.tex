\section{Open Problems}
\label{sec:challenges}

\paragraph{A calibrated belief with an environment-signed likelihood.}
ReBel signs a written belief with the next observation, but a binary one; Agent-BRACE carries certainty, but signed by a calibration target; surprise-triggered mechanisms (Section~\ref{sec:revision}) route nothing to the claim. A belief that carries uncertainty, is scored by the agent's own matured predictions, and receives the error at the claim that made it, without the collapse dense prediction rewards show under group normalisation, is the most direct open problem this survey identifies.

\paragraph{Latent beliefs without ground truth.}
Historical data has no oracle, closed world, or annotation; filter-recovered latents and representation drift are candidate substitutes, unvalidated against each other \citep{dixon2026belief,xue2026representation}; a benchmark pairing a controlled environment with a replay of the same instrument would settle whether belief-level gains transfer.

\paragraph{Belief without action, and belief under governance.}
Revising the belief does not revise a policy conditioned on the old one; action--belief consistency sits far below belief accuracy wherever both are measured \citep{gao2026auditing,deb2026stocktake}. Regulated domains add auditability, which only fixed-space filters provide; LLM-proposed spaces are the candidate bridge \citep{six2026learning,soru2026semantic,chen2026beliefcalibrated,dixon2026model}.
